About

I'm Jacob Israel R. Salazar, based in Manila. For most of the last decade I've worked remotely with teams in Los Angeles, Sydney and Singapore, so async updates, clear handoffs and shipping without a manager in the room are normal for me.

I like owning the whole product, from the schema and the API to the interface and the polish that makes it feel finished. Wamivo is the clearest example: one person, one codebase, shipped and in use.

Outside work I'm making Tinyverse with Hidden Tiny Verse Studio, a hidden-object puzzle game for iOS with hand-drawn watercolor art, written in Swift and SpriteKit. I studied Computer Science at De La Salle University Manila (2014 to 2018).
